**Role:** Team leader · Hanyang Academic Conference, Hanyang University

- Designed and built the **analog front end** for electrooculography (EOG) measurement on a microcontroller.
- Wrote a **MATLAB algorithm** that maps EOG signals to drawing movements, so a user can draw with eye motion only.
- **Grand Award** (Hanyang Academic Conference) and **Best Question Award** (ASKTON) from the University President.
