**Role:** Team leader · COSS Next-Generation Semiconductor MCU Application Competition, Seoul National University (POLARIS)

- Built a bare-metal embedded **34-key digital piano** and flashed firmware directly via USBasp.
- Stored LED control data in flash using `PROGMEM`, enabling updates for **486 NeoPixels per timer interval** — over 10× more than other teams — while keeping animation and piano playback simultaneous under tight MCU memory constraints.
- **1st place, Minister of Education Award**; also received the Achievement Award at Hanyang's Foundation Day Ceremony.
