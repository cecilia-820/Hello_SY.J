  • 2025 – present
    Reference-Free Supply-Locked Ring-Oscillator Temperature Sensor (main research)
    KAIST, Daejeon, Korea

    • Designed a custom standard-cell-based 2-stack LVT ring oscillator as the reference clock. A PLL-based feedback loop maintains a constant shared supply voltage for the LVT and HVT ring oscillators, and temperature is measured from their frequency ratio.
